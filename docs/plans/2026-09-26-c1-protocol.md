# C1 -- the no-fold band on the owner's bar: protocol and instrument

2026-09-26 · branch `wip/hiding-c1` · pre-registration of stage 1's C1 under
deviation HF-1 of `2026-09-25-hiding-feasibility.md` (one run, on the owner's own
account, by the owner's consent, while the owner is away). **No run happens until
the owner names the time.** v3, converged with Codex over three rounds (Appendix). Codex reviews this
document before the instrument is built.

## 1. The question and the verdicts

Can Ice's visual detector tell, on the owner's real bar and with capture active,
that a helper item pushed into the no-fold band is hidden without the fold -- and
that it is back after the spacer collapses -- without the band moving because of the
capture itself?

- **PASS**: a band exists in 600-896 pt (at least one scanned length gives
  `hidden(folded: false)` for the target, followed by `restored` after collapsing),
  and N = 5 full cycles at the midpoint of its widest contiguous run all pass: target
  `hidden(folded: false)`, fold absent, `.protected` and every *templated* owner item
  drawn and no unexplained change in the residual strip (G1's observable
  population), then `restored`. A PASS counts for nothing toward GO; it makes the isolated
  account worth creating (C2).
- **FAIL** (C dropped for this configuration; macOS 27 declared read-only):
  - no scanned length gives `hidden(folded: false)` (every one `stillDrawn`, folded,
    or refused), or
  - the smoke cycles give `unverifiable(contradictsAccessibility)` or any other
    refusal in more than one of five, or the band found in the scan is not hidden at
    its midpoint in the smoke, or
  - a safety stop (section 4).
  Under the owner's one-run consent, a FAIL other than a safety stop is a
  **provisional FAIL**: its replication is a second run that needs a new go and a newly
  named time -- it is never scheduled under this consent. The owner may instead accept
  the provisional FAIL as final.
- **INCONCLUSIVE**: preflight never passes (section 3), or captures stay unreadable;
  re-run at most twice, then *not shown* -- which for C1 counts as FAIL.

## 2. Setup

- **Helpers** (vzhelper, VZGlyphs, never solid blocks), launched in this order so
  that each new item lands at the left end: `Protected` (reference glyph), then the
  **spacer** (a new vzhelper role: one status item resting as Ice rests its control
  item -- `variableLength` plus a chevron image -- with `length <pt>` and `rest`
  commands), then `Target` (target glyph). Intended order, left to right: Target,
  spacer, Protected, then the owner's items.
- Every helper: controller-exit deadman, lifetime cap 900 s, stdin EOF = quit. The
  spacer lives in a helper, not in the controller, so a dead controller collapses it.
- Each launch waits for the helper's `up` acknowledgment and a fresh discovery that
  lists its item before the next helper starts. A relaunch first rests the spacer,
  quits and reaps all three helpers and confirms none of their items is listed.
- `caffeinate -d` for the run's duration only, so the display does not sleep mid-run
  (it ends with the run).
- The capture indicator is warmed up once at the start (24 captures, 0.25 s apart), so
  it is present and stable before the first preflight.
- Baseline, taken once with the spacer **at rest**: Target, the spacer and Protected
  plus every owner item discovery lists, templated from a stable capture with the fold
  absent. The spacer is carried as an observed item (its rest chevron is explained ink
  in every baseline and verification sample, never read as a fold); an owner item the
  detector cannot template (`notObserved`) is watched by the residual-strip comparison
  only. The rest baseline supplies templates only; each expansion's permission comes
  from its own preflight (section 3).
- The check's geometry is C1's own: a C1 discoverer records the spacer's **real** rest
  frame and makes its `minX` the divider; Target is the only section item, Protected is
  supplied explicitly as the reference; the discoverer rejects the read unless Target
  is the sole item left of the divider and Protected lies right of it.

## 3. Preflight -- before every expansion (Codex round 2's conditions)

Taken immediately before `length L`, not the verifier's own preflight: a fresh,
settled, capture-active read (two stable captures at least 1 s apart), compared with
**the roster snapshot** -- the set of listed items, their order and the capture
indicator's frame, recorded at the end of the previous passing reset check (for the
first preflight: at the end of the baseline). A missing snapshot fails closed.

1. the order from AX and from pixels agree: Target, then the spacer, leftmost of every
   rendered item (user and system); Target is the only item left of the spacer;
2. `.protected` and every templated owner item are drawn; the fold is absent;
3. the capture indicator is present and has not moved since the previous read;
4. the roster equals the snapshot (no item appeared, left or reordered).

Fail -> no expansion. Up to 3 attempts within 10 s; still failing -> the run stops as
INCONCLUSIVE (helpers quit, nothing was expanded). If the order is wrong at the very
first preflight, the helpers are relaunched once; wrong again -> INCONCLUSIVE.

The expansion is issued synchronously right after a passing preflight; nothing queued
survives a failed one.

## 4. The latch

One serial capture path: every capture the run takes -- warm-up, baseline, preflight,
the verifier's own sampling, settle, collapse and reset -- goes through a latching
capturer that feeds it to the latch **before** anyone else sees it. A trip sends `rest`
to the spacer synchronously, then quits the helpers. A capture that fails, or an AX read
that comes back late, is itself an immediate abort. The latch watches an **owner-only**
roster (Target and the spacer excluded), plus Protected as a separate one-miss
condition; its miss state resets only after a passing reset check. On **one** credible disappearance of anything but the Target -- a templated
owner item or `.protected` read `notDrawn` in a stable capture, unexplained ink
vanishing from the strip, or the fold appearing while no expansion is in progress --
the spacer is set to rest at once, the helpers quit, the bar is re-read until it
matches the baseline, and the run ends as a **safety stop** (FAIL, not replicated). If
the bar has not matched the baseline after 30 s, that is recorded as a safety stop
**needing the owner's attention**, and the report says so first. Any unreadable or late observation during a hold aborts before the next
expansion.

## 5. Sequence

1. Warm-up, baseline, first preflight (no expansion yet): the "dry" minute. Anything
   off -> INCONCLUSIVE, stop.
2. **Scan**: for L = 600, 616, ..., 896 (19 lengths): preflight -> jump from rest to L
   -> settle (two stable captures >= 1 s apart, start >= 1 s after the jump, within
   10 s) -> verdicts: Target (`hidden(folded:)` / `stillDrawn` / refusal), fold,
   latch -> collapse to rest -> settle -> Target `restored`? -> **reset check**: the bar
   is *baseline-equivalent* (below); a mismatch is a safety stop.
3. **Smoke**: the midpoint of the widest contiguous run of `hidden(folded: false)`
   lengths; 5 cycles of step 2 at that length.
4. Teardown: rest, quit and reap the helpers, stop `caffeinate`; helper preference
   domains empty; the bar -- now without the helpers -- must be baseline-equivalent
   for the owner population within 30 s, else a safety stop needing the owner's
   attention, never a normal end. A teardown mismatch turns an otherwise PASS into a
   safety stop.

**Baseline-equivalent** (the reset check and the teardown): every templated owner item
drawn within 2 pt of its baseline x; the residual strip (the untemplated owner items
and the gaps) without unexplained ink change against the baseline; the capture
indicator at its baseline frame; the fold absent; for the reset check also Target, the
spacer (at rest) and Protected at their baseline x. The roster snapshot is taken only
from a baseline-equivalent reading.

About 5-8 minutes.

## 6. Evidence

Per capture only verdicts, frames and times go to the evidence directory; strip
images are kept only for the helpers' region, and deleted after the verdicts are
written unless the owner asks to keep them. Output that names apps stays in the
evidence directory. No recording.

## Amendment v4 -- after the Codex review of the built instrument (2026-09-26)

The instrument as first built left the fold and the untemplated owner items unwatched
(both found in review). Amended, with Codex's corrections:

- **The fold in the latch**: observed through a bracketed read (capture -> AX ->
  capture, stable), never one image plus one AX read. The stage keeps an *expansion
  window* flag, set synchronously before `length` and cleared only once rest is
  re-established after the collapse; a credible `present` fold while the window is
  closed trips the latch. An AX read that fails or comes back late is a capture failure
  (abort).
- **Untemplated owner items** are watched through **keyed** discovery (a fresh
  `MenuBarDiscoverer` pass, by `ItemKey`), not by residual ink: in every latch read,
  the reset check and teardown each must stay listed with its AX `minX` within 2 pt of
  baseline; a lagging or ambiguous read fails closed. This is weaker evidence than
  pixels -- "listed and stationary", not "drawn" -- and section 1's PASS and section 5's
  baseline-equivalence say so for these items; templated items keep the pixel
  criterion.
- **Preflight order**: the pixel order is compared with the AX order projected onto the
  templated items; untemplated items are checked by the keyed conditions above. The
  roster snapshot is the fresh full AX roster, taken only after a passing reset.
- **Teardown**: a helper preference domain that is not empty, or cannot be read, is a
  teardown failure -- a safety stop needing the owner's attention.

## Amendment v5 -- after live-review round 5 (2026-09-26)

Round 5 confirmed the three round-4 P0s resolved. An independent cross-check then found,
and Codex confirmed (round 5b), nine defects -- three of which made PASS unreachable on
any real run, so the one approved run would have been wasted. Rulings (all adopted):

- **Rest confirmation** (P0): the post-collapse read uses Protected as its reference
  (an empty reference set is never stable), starts >= 1 s after `rest`, needs two
  stable captures >= 1 s apart within 10 s, and requires the spacer drawn within
  tolerance of its own rest-baseline x.
- **Owner/Protected drawn check** (P0): Protected as the reference; an unstable or
  failed read fails closed.
- **Teardown latch** (P0): before cleanup quits the helpers, the latch switches to a
  helpers-torn-down mode -- no Protected condition, all three helper ids out of the
  owner checks, owner templated and keyed checks kept. Protected is never an owner item.
- **Escalation** (P0): a teardown mismatch or failed reap after an earlier safety stop
  raises it to *needing the owner's attention*; the first terminal reason stands and
  cleanup does not run twice.
- **Keyed checkpoints** (P0): every keyed-check failure, at a cycle checkpoint or a
  preflight, feeds the latch at once.
- **Keyed discovery bound** (P0): one single-flight, deadline-bounded discovery
  executor; a timeout marks it stuck for the rest of the run, latches a capture failure
  at once (rest, then quit), makes every later pass fail immediately, and is recorded.
  A reap that cannot confirm is a safety stop needing attention.
- **Signals** (P0): a signal is its own terminal reason (needing attention), arms the
  same recorded backstop first, and records a verdict before exit.
- **Thread affinity** (P0): terminal handling off the main thread does only the
  thread-safe rest and quit; the reap and teardown stay with the main thread (under the
  watchdog's backstop). Nothing off the main thread runs the main run loop.
- **caffeinate** (P1): `caffeinate -d -w <vizprobe pid>`; the backstop also stops it.

**I7 -- the orchestration test** (new; required before the run): the real C1 stage code,
through injected seams (capturer, discoverer, AX readers, helper launcher/channel,
clock/sleep, caffeinate, preference domains, evidence), driven against a fake bar in
which the helpers exist until quit, the spacer rests or expands, Protected is a stable
reference and the Target is drawn or hidden per scenario. Minimum scenarios: (1) stable
all-hidden scan plus five smoke cycles -> PASS; (2) the Target never hides ->
PROVISIONAL FAIL; (3) each latch trigger, an unconfirmed rest and a failed reset check
-> `rest` before quit, SAFETY STOP; (4) a teardown mismatch after an earlier stop ->
SAFETY STOP NEEDING ATTENTION; (5) `--dry` sends no `length` and, on a clean bar, ends
PROVISIONAL FAIL (the Target never hides), never PASS; (6) the fake's helper lifecycle
and Protected reference are exercised, not bypassed. Acceptance for the instrument adds:
I7 green.

## Amendment v6 -- after the cross-check of rework #5 (2026-09-26)

A second independent cross-check (18 verified findings) and Codex round 6a agreed on
this fix set. Two of the defects again made PASS unreachable on the owner's real bar,
which has several items the baseline cannot template.

- **Fold references** (P0): every fold read during the cycles uses only templated ids
  (the helpers' and the templated owner items') as references; the other listed items
  are still sampled. An empty reference set fails closed.
- **Staged teardown** (P0; amends section 5 step 4's order): rest -> quit and reap
  Target and the spacer -> a settled read with Protected as the only reference (it is
  then the leftmost item): the fold must be absent -> quit and reap Protected -> the
  owner-population checks (templated pixels within 2 pt, untemplated items listed and
  stationary, the indicator) without another fold read -> preference domains empty. A
  failure at either stage is a teardown mismatch. No cleanup path may quit Protected
  before the fold read.
- **Fresh verification baseline** (P0): the verifier's baseline expires after 600 s.
  Before a cycle's preflight, at confirmed rest, it is re-prepared whenever its age plus
  a conservative estimate of the next cycle (a fixed initial estimate for the first
  cycle, then the measured one with margin) would pass the limit less a margin; the
  prediction is recorded; freshness is asserted before every verify; a failed
  re-prepare ends the run through teardown.
- **Unstable is not failed** (P1): only an absent observation (capture or AX failure)
  latches a capture failure; a successful but unstable or disagreeing pair is recorded
  and retried within the existing budgets (rest confirmation: 10 s; preflight: 3
  attempts).
- **Setup obeys a stop** (P0): the terminal state is checked before each setup step and
  before and after each helper launch; every exit after the first launch goes through
  the full teardown and accounting; a recorded safety stop is never replaced by
  INCONCLUSIVE, and a teardown failure after it escalates.
- **All discovery bounded** (P1): every discovery pass after the first launch goes
  through the one bounded executor; a timeout trips, and no caller uses a result once
  the run is terminal.
- **I7 extended**: a realistic fake bar (several owner items, untemplated ones among
  them, one left of the leftmost templated item; also a bar with no templated owner
  item); fault knobs armed by phase after the baseline; one test per trigger (templated
  owner miss, Protected miss, untemplated vanish, untemplated shift, fold without
  expansion, capture failure, discovery timeout, unconfirmed rest, reset-check failure,
  trip then teardown mismatch, a signal during setup), each asserting the exact first
  terminal reason, the verdict, and rest before the first quit; a virtual clock advanced
  by every sleep and by measured capture, discovery and preparation latencies.
- **Run time**: the owner is told the duration the virtual-clock run of scenario 1
  measures, not an estimate; it must fit under the 15-minute watchdog with an explicit
  margin, or the watchdog or the refresh policy is revisited before the run.

## Amendment v7 -- after live-review round 7 and the rework #6 cross-check (2026-09-26)

Codex round 7 and a third independent cross-check (Codex round 7b converged on the fix
set). Once more a defect passed every test and would have failed only live: the live
discoverer reports its own read as not read (the harness filters itself out), which
the verifier's plan refuses, so every live run would have ended INCONCLUSIVE at step 3.

- **Own read** (P0): C1's discoverer composes its set as read (C1 supplies the divider
  itself); the fakes model the raw live seam (not read, self filtered).
- **Baselines before prepare** (P0): the owner baselines (templates, untemplated,
  indicator, roster) are seeded before the verifier is prepared, so an abort at prepare
  ends INCONCLUSIVE, not in a false teardown mismatch.
- **Dynamic owner items** (P1): an owner template the baseline marks dynamic leaves the
  pixel population (latch, fold references, drawn checks, reset, teardown) and is
  watched through the keyed condition; its fallback is recorded. The latch trips on a
  templated owner item only when it is absent, or uniquely matched off its baseline x,
  in a capture stable against Protected (or two consecutive agreeing captures); a weak
  or ambiguous match alone never trips. A baseline-static item that later reads weak or
  ambiguous at a reset check or teardown stays a mismatch.
- **Post-rest fold** (P1): the fold read after rest is fed to the latch.
- **caffeinate** (P1; amends section 5 step 4's order): stopped once at the very end
  (and by the backstop), after every verdict-deciding read -- never by cleanup. Off the
  main thread, terminal handling sends rest and non-blocking quit requests only.
- **Freshness enforced** (P1): the verifier baseline's age is checked before every
  verify call (hidden and restored); a stale baseline ends the run through teardown as
  INCONCLUSIVE.
- **Time budget** (P1): the virtual clock is calibrated per whole operation to the
  owner-bar measurements (observe ~0.43 s, verify ~7.2 s, prepare ~11.5 s, discovery
  pass ~36.5 ms, each latched capture adding one pass), without double-counting modelled
  sleeps. Before each cycle the stage projects the rest of the run (measured cycle
  times, remaining cycles, one possible re-prepare, a teardown allowance) and, if it
  would pass the watchdog less a margin, ends cleanly through teardown as INCONCLUSIVE.
  **The watchdog is 20 minutes**; the helpers' lifetime cap is derived from the same
  setting (watchdog + backstop + margin).
- **I7**: exact verdict asserted in every fault case; a truly bounded injected executor
  for the timeout case; a refresh-threshold regression; a prepare-not-ready case; a
  dynamic owner item that redraws mid-run -> PASS; a baseline-static item that changes
  appearance -> SAFETY STOP; a slow-discovery case (290 ms passes) -> INCONCLUSIVE
  before the watchdog; and a written audit of every fake seam against the live one.
- **Dry rehearsal** (recommended to the owner, not yet agreed): immediately before the
  real run, in the same sitting, `--dry` on the owner's bar (helpers up, no `length`,
  all cycles, full teardown); the real run starts only if it ends PROVISIONAL FAIL with
  no setup abort, no safety stop, an equivalent teardown and a duration within budget.
  It checks the live wiring, not the expansion.

## Amendment v8 -- the helpers do not land leftmost on this bar (2026-09-26)

Codex round 8 converged on the code; a fourth cross-check then showed that section 2's
premise ("each new item lands at the left end") is false on the owner's bar: in every
recorded run the helpers landed right of 1-3 owner items (FINDINGS: "a new item does
not reliably appear at the left end"). Expanding with an owner item left of the spacer
would displace the owner's item, so C1 as written can never reach an expansion here.
Codex round 8b agreed:

- **Parked items** (P0): items the catalogue classifies as parked (frames below the
  bar; 2-3 always present here) are not owner items: out of the divider count, the owner
  AX ids and the untemplated candidates. Stacked and frameless items still fail closed;
  the three helpers must be on the bar.
- **Placement gate** (P0): right after the launches, discovery and pixels must show
  Target -> spacer -> Protected left of every on-bar owner item; otherwise the helpers
  are reaped, their domains verified empty, and the run ends **INCONCLUSIVE: helpers not
  leftmost: N owner items left** -- not a C1 failure. No expansion before this gate.
- **Teardown fold rule** (P0): if no `length` was ever sent, the Protected-only fold read
  is recorded but is not a teardown mismatch (C1 cannot have caused a fold);
  baseline-equivalence stays authoritative. After any `length`, the gate guarantees the
  helpers were leftmost, so the Protected-referenced read applies.
- **Placement by the helpers' own preferred position** (option A; pending the owner):
  before each launch, write `NSStatusItem Preferred Position <unique autosave name>` in
  that helper's own domain only, computed from a fresh discovery; verify placement
  through the gate; forget the keys at teardown and verify the domains empty. Unproven
  on macOS 27 until a placement-only dry rehearsal succeeds. If the gate still fails,
  C1 cannot run on this bar; the only remaining path is the isolated account, by new
  owner direction.
