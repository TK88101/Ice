## Design

**Rule.** While Ice rests with the hidden length applied, every slot of a hidden-section
member that `MenuBarAgent` lays out on any bar is covered by a panel showing what lies
below the `Menubar` window there. Where the spacer already hides (the built-in's band)
no slot is listed and nothing is covered. One rule for every display; no per-display
length, no measured table.

D1 **Where (no capture).** `MenuBarAgent`'s tree is read (F2) on a queue of its own, once
a second while Ice rests and at once on a length change or a change of the active
display: per bar, the slots and their owners. The tree only proposes; a hit test decides:
- *Candidates* (IceCore `CoverPlan`, pure): the slots, in any window with that bar's
  frame, whose owner is a **coverable** member: a pid all of whose discovered items are
  hidden-section members. A pid that also owns a visible-section item is never covered
  (the tree gives a slot's owner, not which of its items it is): limit L10.
- *Verified*: each candidate's centre is hit-tested through `MenuBarAgent`'s element
  (F4); it stands only if the hit returns that owner. A stale window's slot (F3), a
  stacked slot behind a `«`, a slot under another app's window fail and are not covered.
  Verification runs when the candidate set of a bar changes, at activation, on an
  active-display change, and every 10 s; for its few milliseconds that bar's panels
  ignore mouse events (F4), then take them again.
- *Blocks*: the verified slots, merged when 4 pt or less apart; each side padded by up
  to 4 pt (F8's 3 pt) but never into a slot that is not a verified member's, and clipped
  to the bar outside the notch. One panel per block, no cap.
Every read and verification carries the controller's generation; a result of an older
generation is dropped.

D2 **What is drawn (one capture per background).** The cover draws the pixels below the
`Menubar` window (F5, F7) for the bar's whole strip, kept per display and cut to each
block. It is captured again only when the background may have changed: first use, the
display's current Space, light/dark, display parameters, wake, unlock, screen saver end,
the wallpaper store's modification date, a change in the set (ids, bounds, order) of
on-screen windows below `Menubar` that reach into the bar's strip (an app's window under
the transparent bar; read from the window list, no capture), and every 30 minutes while
a cover is up. One budget for the whole app, on one serial queue, counts every
WindowServer image call: at most four calls within one second, then none for 12 s (F8;
IceCore `CoverCaptureBudget`); T3 measures that exact shape. While a capture is owed,
the last image of that display is used. Each session also takes the on-screen strip and compares it with the
below-strip over a slot-free stretch next to the block: if they differ (mean over 6 of
255), the bar is not transparent there and that display is not covered (said in the
layout pane and the log).

D3 **The panel.** One borderless non-activating panel per block: level `.statusBar`,
opaque, no shadow, takes mouse events (a click on it is a click on empty bar space, F10
unchanged; nothing reaches the item beneath), `[.canJoinAllSpaces, .stationary,
.ignoresCycle, .fullScreenNone]`, not an accessibility element. No hit test is needed to
follow the items (D1 reads the tree), so it never has to let clicks through.

D4 **When.** Off before the length goes: on a retire (arranging with Command, a drag, a
blocked precondition, a member change) the coordinator removes the panels synchronously
and bumps the generation, then sets the standard length, so the owner always arranges
real items and no panel outlives its layout. On: after a length is applied, the first
panel appears only from a read and verification made after it, in the current
generation. The IceBar being open changes nothing.

D5 **The active display.** In the unverified mode a change of the active display alone
(same items, same members) keeps the length (F9 would drop the cover for 1-2 s at every
click on the other display). The panels stay while the bars are read and verified again
at once (D1); removing them first would show the members at every such click. The
verified mode is unchanged.

D6 **Out.** The previous plan's T2d (no hiding with two displays): `DisplayTopologyTracker`,
the two block reasons, their strings, the coordinator's use of them. Kept: the lab
`length` commands, the probes, the records.

D7 **Code placement.** IceCore: `CoverPlan`, `CoverCaptureBudget`, the machine's D5.
A new local package `Packages/MenuBarCover` (AppKit, ApplicationServices; depends on
IceCore): the tree reader behind a protocol, the background, the panel, the controller,
and a CLI `mbcover`. The app: the coordinator drives the controller. The Xcode project
gains the package reference and product (as IceCore and MenuBarDiscovery are wired).
